Fiber optics

How do you test fiber links?

We use OTDR and insertion-loss testing to verify splice quality, link loss, and end-to-end performance before handover.

Can you support campus backbone upgrades?

Yes. Our engineering team designs phased migration plans to minimize disruption while expanding capacity and resilience.

Cybersecurity

What is included in a security audit?

We review network segmentation, endpoint protection, identity controls, logging, and business-critical policies against your current risk posture.

Do you support compliance reporting?

Yes. We provide documentation and remediation guidance aligned to common governance frameworks and operational controls.
