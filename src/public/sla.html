Service level agreements

Our support commitments are structured around measurable uptime guarantees, scheduled maintenance windows, and ongoing security oversight.

Uptime guarantees

  • 99.9% service availability for managed connectivity and network operations.
  • Priority response windows for severity 1 incidents within 15 minutes.
  • Monthly service reports detailing availability, incidents, and trend analysis.

Maintenance contracts

  • Scheduled maintenance windows agreed in advance with client stakeholders.
  • Planned patching and configuration review for critical systems.
  • Post-maintenance validation and documented change records.

Security audits

  • Quarterly security reviews for managed environments and infrastructure.
  • Vulnerability assessments and remediation recommendations.
  • Evidence-backed audit trails for compliance and governance reporting.
