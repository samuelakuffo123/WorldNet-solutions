Networks & fiber

What kinds of networks do you build?

We design and implement LAN, WAN, MAN and enterprise networks over radio and fiber, with structured cabling in UTP, STP and fiber. Our work spans offices, campuses, banks, ISPs and long-haul corridors — including the Eastern Corridor fiber project from Accra to Bawku.

Can you support campus backbone upgrades?

Yes. We have delivered backbone links and campus networks (for example KNUST Sunyani campus) using phased migration plans to minimize disruption while expanding capacity and resilience.

Security & support

What security services do you provide?

Systems security, anti-virus support, firewall and VPN configuration (SonicWall, Cyberoam, Smoothwall), plus physical security & surveillance for offices and campuses.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes. We provide servicing & maintenance contracts for computers and peripherals, field and onsite engineering, remote support, and printer, monitor and UPS repairs.
