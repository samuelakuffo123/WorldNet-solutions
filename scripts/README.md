## 📖 The Story

WorldNet ICT Solutions has been powering Ghana's IT infrastructure—handling complex network installations, cybersecurity, and server management—but without a website that reflects their expertise. We're changing that.

We're a team of seven software engineering students from the University of Ghana, building a professional web portal that gives WorldNet a digital home. It's a clean, modern platform that builds trust, showcases expertise, and makes it easy for enterprise clients to find IT solutions.

## 🎯 What We're Solving

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| No online presence | Professional, trust-building website |

## 🚀 What We're Building

### 1. Service Catalog
Organized browsing of WorldNet's services with detailed technical specs, hardware preferences, and methodologies.

### 2. Lead Capture System
Smart forms for service tiers, infrastructure needs, and consultations. Inquiries auto-route to management via email.

### 3. Portfolio Showcase
Visual dashboard featuring past projects, installations, case studies, and client success stories.

### 4. SLA & FAQ Section
Clear documentation on uptime guarantees, maintenance contracts, security audits, and technical FAQs.
